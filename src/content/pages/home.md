I believe in change. I believe that change is available to all. I believe that change occurs on a constant basis. Our choice is not if we change but how we change.

Life can be hard, things hurt, we hurt, and we unintentionally hurt others. When we are in the depths of hurt it is easy to feel like things have always been this way and will always be this way. Luckily, feelings are not facts!

My hope is that together we can identify ineffective interactions and the patterns that keep them alive. Together we will intentionally elicit new behaviors that no longer invite pain and inefficiency, but use your roots to gather the nourishment you need for growth and change.
