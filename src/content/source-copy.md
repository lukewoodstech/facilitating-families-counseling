## Eating Disorders
URL: https://sites.google.com/view/facilitatingfamiliescounseling/scope-of-practice/eating-disorders
Page title: `FFC LLC - Eating Disorders`

# Eating Disorders

### Anorexia, Bulimia, and Orthoxia

Change does happen with these diagnoses. The first thing I identify is medical stability. If the patient is not medically stable, outpatient treatment is not an appropriate level of care. I will recommend inpatient treatment and will work with the patient as they access this care. When their body is medically stable, I will continue to provide therapeutic care. Patients that are medically stable are able to make significant changes with outpatient treatment.

I recognize that recovering from an eating disorder requires symptom interruption. Interrupting symptomatic behaviors can be exhausting. I don't want you worn out and not having the ability to fight that nagging voice of an eating disorder. Together we look at the sustainability of interrupting the symptoms necessary to escape an eating disorder.

The constant barrage of negativity in your head, that is so demeaning and constraining is not you, and is not true! That voice is not your child, it is not your spouse, and it is not your parent. That's the voice of an eating disorder and it has you, or your loved one under its influence. The most common denominator of eating disorders is this voice.

I learned how to recognize that voice while working with many patients in an inpatient program. I'm not afraid of the voice and you don't have to be either. My goal for you is that you are able to identify that voice and make the changes needed to separate it from the authentic you.

### Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder(ARFID)

I have worked with ARFID impacting patients who;
1) have had an experience with food that caused them to be fearful ie choking allergies, or medical issues.
2) with individuals who lack the hormone ghrelin.
3) with individuals who have heightened sensory issues. These sensory issues often are co-occurring with autism spectrum behaviors.

### Involving Others in Care

The age of the patient and their ability to nourish themselves is significant in treatment planning.

When a young child is experiencing an eating disorder the child is not capable of providing nutrition for themselves. Education, training, and support is focused at the parental level.

The Maudsley approach for teanagers with eating disorders also relies on parental education, training, and support.

Eating disorders often divide those struggling from partners or parents who want to support recovery. It is important to know your enemies and friends. Eating disorders are not friends, even if they are comfortable.

Change is not comfortable as an individual or as a family. It is difficult to initiate and encourage needed change. I often remind families that their voice needs to be louder than the voice of an eating disorder.

## Couples and Family
URL: https://sites.google.com/view/facilitatingfamiliescounseling/scope-of-practice/couples-and-family
Page title: `FFC LLC - Couples and Family`

# Couples and Family

I enjoy working with all phases of the family developmental cycle not limited to but including: premarital, newly married, married without children, married with young children, blended families, families with teens, families with young adults, families launching children, empty nest adjustments, parenting adult children, aging parents, and family reunification.

All families are different. Families are not linear, and are frequently found in more than one stage at a time. Families are fabulous.

Families are made up of subsystems. Patterns develop within these subsystems and then become the way families operate even when it is not the way we intended. I focus on correcting ineffective spousal, parental, executive and sibling subsystems.

When subsystems are operating effectively the family becomes a shield to stress. When subsystems are ineffective stress will compromise family strengths. We work to build families that solve problems rather than families that are the source of problems.

I am comfortable working with high conflict couples. This high level of conflict is not beneficial to individuals or the family as a whole. Addressing the changes needed to restore effective family interaction occurs after understanding why behaviors manifest.

# Individual Support

I am a strength based therapist. You have Strengths. You have always had strengths. When they are silenced by other intrusive patterns, you doubt you ever had them. Through exploration and fact checking, we will activate behaviors, beliefs, and thinking patterns that rejuvenate and nourish these strengths.

It is my goal to create a connection with you where you feel safe and can share with me unwanted patterns. Patterns change. Your patterns will change. Together, we validate those patterns and identify the best way to invite change.

I believe in being upfront, honest, and direct in identifying what is and is not working for you as an individual. We are a team. We are working together to bring about the change you deserve.

Trauma does not have to keep playing out in your brain or in your life or in your relationships.
