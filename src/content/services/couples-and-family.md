I enjoy working with all phases of the family developmental cycle not limited to but including: premarital, newly married, married without children, married with young children, blended families, families with teens, families with young adults, families launching children, empty nest adjustments, parenting adult children, aging parents, and family reunification.

All families are different. Families are not linear, and are frequently found in more than one stage at a time. Families are fabulous.

Families are made up of subsystems. Patterns develop within these subsystems and then become the way families operate even when it is not the way we intended. I focus on correcting ineffective spousal, parental, executive and sibling subsystems.

When subsystems are operating effectively the family becomes a shield to stress. When subsystems are ineffective stress will compromise family strengths. We work to build families that solve problems rather than families that are the source of problems.

I am comfortable working with high conflict couples. This high level of conflict is not beneficial to individuals or the family as a whole. Addressing the changes needed to restore effective family interaction occurs after understanding why behaviors manifest.
